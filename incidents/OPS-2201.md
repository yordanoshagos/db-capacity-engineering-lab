# OPS-2201 — Patient name search unusably slow at shift change

| Field        | Value                                    |
|--------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Priority** | P2 — High                                |
| **Status**   | Open                                     |
| **Reported by** | Night Charge Nurse (via Help Desk)    |
| **Component** | `patient-lookup`                        |
| **Affected endpoint** | `GET /api/patients/search`      |

## Report (in the reporter's words)

> "Every night around shift change, when all of us are pulling up our patients
> at the same time, the **search by last name** just spins. Sometimes it takes
> ten-plus seconds, sometimes it errors out. If it's quiet and I search on my
> own it comes back instantly. The **'recent patients'** panel on the same
> screen is always fast, even when search is dying. It's only search that's
> bad, and only when a lot of us are on it."

## What we know

- Single searches on an idle system feel fine.
- The problem scales with the number of people searching simultaneously.
- Other endpoints on the same service are unaffected during the slowdown.
- The dataset is ~100,000 patient records.

> ⚠️ **This is a field hypothesis, not a verdict.** The suspected cause and
> symptoms above come from whoever filed the ticket — they may be incomplete or
> simply wrong. Your job is to find what is *actually* happening. Disproving the
> reported cause with evidence is a full-marks answer, not a failure.

## Your task

1. Baseline the system (`load-tests/00-baseline.js`).
2. Reproduce the shift-change burst: `k6 run load-tests/reproduce-OPS-2201.js`.
3. Figure out what the database is doing for each search request and why it gets
   dramatically worse under concurrency but fine when isolated.
4. Propose and apply a fix, then re-run the reproduction and compare.
5. Document everything in `LAB_JOURNAL.md` (Investigation OPS-2201).

_Hint on where to look, not what you'll find: the database can tell you exactly
how it executes a query._
