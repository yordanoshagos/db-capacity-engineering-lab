# OPS-2202 — Whole app freezes during registration surges (but the DB looks idle)

| Field        | Value                                    |
|--------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Priority** | P1 — Critical                            |
| **Status**   | Open                                     |
| **Reported by** | On-call SRE                           |
| **Component** | `capacity-api`                          |
| **Affected endpoint** | Effectively all read endpoints, incl. `GET /api/patients/recent` |

## Report (in the reporter's words)

> "During the morning registration surge the **entire API** grinds to a halt.
> Even the tiny 'recent patients' call — which is a trivial query — takes
> seconds or returns 500s. Here's the weird part: the **database CPU and disk
> are basically flat**. The DBAs pulled up their dashboards and said 'it's not
> us, the DB is bored.' Yet the app is clearly waiting on *something*
> database-related. As soon as traffic drops, everything recovers instantly. No
> deploy went out, no data changed."

## What we know

- Fails under a sudden spike of concurrent requests.
- Even the cheapest, fastest query is affected — it's not a slow-query problem.
- Database server resource usage stays **low** during the incident.
- Recovers on its own when concurrency falls.

> ⚠️ **This is a field hypothesis, not a verdict.** The suspected cause and
> symptoms above come from whoever filed the ticket — they may be incomplete or
> simply wrong. Your job is to find what is *actually* happening. Disproving the
> reported cause with evidence is a full-marks answer, not a failure.

## Your task

1. Baseline the system (`load-tests/00-baseline.js`).
2. Reproduce the surge: `k6 run load-tests/reproduce-OPS-2202.js`.
3. Reconcile the paradox: the DB is idle, the query is trivial, yet requests
   pile up. Where is the time actually being spent between the request arriving
   and the query executing?
4. Propose and apply a fix, then re-run and compare throughput/error rate.
5. Document everything in `LAB_JOURNAL.md` (Investigation OPS-2202).

_Hint on where to look, not what you'll find: a query can't run until it has
something it needs from the application tier first._
